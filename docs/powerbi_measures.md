# TeleConnect Power BI DAX Measures Specification

This document provides production-ready Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) measures for building the **TeleConnect Customer Churn & Retention Intelligence Dashboard** in Power BI.

---

## 1. Core Volume & Churn KPI Measures

### Total Customers
```dax
Total Customers = 
DISTINCTCOUNT('customers'[customer_id])
```
*Description:* Counts total unique customer accounts.

### Churned Customers
```dax
Churned Customers = 
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT('customers'[customer_id]),
    'customers'[churn] = "Yes"
)
```
*Description:* Counts total accounts that have cancelled service.

### Active Customers
```dax
Active Customers = 
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT('customers'[customer_id]),
    'customers'[churn] = "No"
)
```
*Description:* Counts total accounts currently active.

### Churn Rate %
```dax
Churn Rate % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Churned Customers],
    [Total Customers],
    0
) * 100
```
*Description:* Percentage of total customer base that has churned. Format as `0.00%`.

## 2. Revenue & Financial Impact Measures

### Average Monthly Charges
```dax
Avg Monthly Charge = 
AVERAGE('customers'[monthly_charges])
```
*Description:* Average monthly subscription charge per customer ($).

### Average Customer Tenure
```dax
Avg Tenure Months = 
AVERAGE('customers'[tenure_months])
```
*Description:* Average tenure length in months across accounts.

### Total Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
```dax
Total Monthly Revenue = 
SUM('customers'[monthly_charges])
```
*Description:* Total current monthly revenue generated by all customers ($).

### Total Annualized Revenue (ARR)
```dax
Total Annualized Revenue = 
[Total Monthly Revenue] * 12
```
*Description:* Total estimated annual revenue across all accounts ($).

### Annualized Revenue at Risk
```dax
Annualized Revenue at Risk = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('customers'[estimated_annual_revenue]),
    'customers'[churn] = "Yes"
)
```
*Description:* Estimated annual revenue lost specifically due to churned accounts ($).

### % Revenue at Risk
```dax
% Revenue at Risk = 
DIVIDE(
    [Annualized Revenue at Risk],
    [Total Annualized Revenue],
    0
) * 100
```
*Description:* Proportion of total annual revenue lost to churn.

---

## 3. Advanced Risk & Segmentation Measures

### High-Risk Customer Count
```dax
High-Risk Customer Count = 
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT('customers'[customer_id]),
    'customers'[contract_type] = "Month-to-month",
    'customers'[support_tickets] >= 3,
    'customers'[churn] = "No"
)
```
*Description:* Counts active customers currently on Month-to-month contracts with 3+ support tickets who are at imminent risk of churning.

### High-Value Churned Revenue
```dax
High-Value Churned Revenue = 
CALCULATE(
    [Annualized Revenue at Risk],
    'customers'[monthly_charges] >= 80.00
)
```
*Description:* Annual revenue lost from high-tier ($80+/mo) churned customers.

### Average CSAT - Churned vs Active
```dax
Avg CSAT Churned = 
CALCULATE(
    AVERAGE('customers'[satisfaction_score]),
    'customers'[churn] = "Yes"
)
```
*Description:* Average satisfaction score among churned accounts.
